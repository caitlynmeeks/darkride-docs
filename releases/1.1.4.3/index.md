# What's new in 1.1.4.3

Darkride 1.1.4.3 beta is on [GitHub](https://github.com/caitlynmeeks/darkride-interface/releases/tag/v1.1.4.3-beta).
For what changed in 1.1.4.2, see [What's new in 1.1.4.2](/releases/1.1.4.2/); for what doesn't work yet, see
[Known issues](/known-issues).

**Darkride 1.1.4.3 includes security fixes and stability improvements.** We recommend everyone update. Your settings
carry over from 1.1.4.2.

## The Script Log is back

Choose **Developer** > **Scripting** > **Script Log…** to see your scripts' output in a window of its own, dark like
the panels, each line with its time and the script that printed it. It had been missing since 1.1.0. Darkride's own
scripts are hidden until you choose **Developer** > **Script Log: Show Default Scripts**.

## Fixed

- On Windows, Darkride no longer freezes while it starts, and it starts even when the network is bad.
- On Windows, Darkride no longer closes after your computer wakes from sleep.
- A docked **Explore** now closes when you **Hop**.

## Your own world server

If you host your own world, update your world server: it's on the
[1.1.4.5 server release](https://github.com/caitlynmeeks/darkride-interface/releases/tag/v1.1.4.5-server-beta). See [Update to a new
version](/worlds/your-own-server#update-to-a-new-version).
