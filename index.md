# Darkride docs

Guides for people who build in Darkride.

- [Put up a world](/worlds/put-up-a-world): make a world, build it, bring in an old High Fidelity world, and invite
  your friends. It runs on Darkride's servers.
- [Run your own world server](/worlds/your-own-server): run a world on your own computer or a rented server.
- [Making bots for Darkride](/bots/guide) (draft): bot accounts, running a bot beside your world, and writing its
  script.

More guides are on the way: getting started, building a world, and avatars.

Something wrong or missing? Every page can be fixed by anyone: see
[how to suggest a fix](https://github.com/caitlynmeeks/darkride-docs#suggest-a-fix).
